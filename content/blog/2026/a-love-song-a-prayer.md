Lately I've been thinking about death a lot. More than maybe I should, but that doesn't matter. It's a compulsion, a fascination that I just can't seem to shake. But since we all die, we ought not to avoid thinking about it, or to fear it. What entrances me about death is that, although to die is to commit a great sin, in the eyes of the westerner, there can simply be no aesthetic value conferred on existence nor its cessation. If it was right just for something to exist, and conversely wrong for something not to exist, then humanity should cease to function! We can only be saddened by the loss of accompaniment which arrives upon someone's departure, and nothing more.

~ ~ ~ Even though I could never expect a funeral to follow my death, and I advise against it in my will, 


Indecisive as ever, I can narrow my choice down to as few as five genres: *a love song; a prayer; a ballad; a dance number; and a blues*.

*A love song*&nbsp;&nbsp;•&nbsp;&nbsp;Shirley Horn's recording of *Come a little closer / Wild is the wind* is my favourite love song. It touches, mesmerises, transforms, destroys, revitalises, and caresses me. It's the sound of longing.

*A prayer*&nbsp;&nbsp;•&nbsp;&nbsp;Let John Coltrane sing over *I Want to Talk About You*, and everything will be okay. 

*A ballad*&nbsp;&nbsp;•&nbsp;&nbsp;What better choice for a ballad than *Echoes*? Roger pens my favourite lyrics ever in this song: *Strangers passing in the street / by chance two separate glances meet / and I am you and what I see is me / And do I take you by the hand / and lead you through the land / and help me understand the best I can?*.

*A dance number*&nbsp;&nbsp;•&nbsp;&nbsp;Aretha Franklin's *Ramblin'*. Because, wouldn't it just be so funny? A huge, surging big band sound for a tiny funeral. I think it would make my mother laugh, because she knows the lyrics. If you know me you know I don't dance, although in another life I would hope this isn't true. I am just not compelled by anything to want to dance, just as you may not be compelled by anything to want to listen to free jazz or plane lumber.

*A blues*&nbsp;&nbsp;•&nbsp;&nbsp;
