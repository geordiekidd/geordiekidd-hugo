Ngwerr ayeng mer atyengenh-warn alpek, Melbourne-warn Nthwereyeng. Ayeng kalty-irretyam Anmatyerr 4 monthsek apek. Rrantherr ayeng ingkerrek kalty-anthemel anngampelhilemel irlparrernerrewarrek. Rrantherr wetyek mparewarrek, tyernp angketyam atyeng thang yarn wetyemeng. Rrantherr kalty-antherrewarrek tyerrty map-akert, mer-akert, nengkerr-akert, altyerr-akert, ayeng rlterrk-ilek angkety arrantherrenh-henhel. 

*Yesterday I returned to my home, to Melbourne from Ti Tree Station (Nturiya). I have been learning Anmatyerr for about 4 months. You all taught me everything, opened my eyes, and opened my ears. You made me listen, and talked to me slowly when I couldn't understand. You taught me about people, about country, about dreaming, about culture, and you made me strong with your language.*
