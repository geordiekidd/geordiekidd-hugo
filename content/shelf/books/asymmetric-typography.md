For a book on design it leans very heavily on prose. But it is so illuminating on the graphics it does include. A truly wonderful essay on the use of space, proportion, and weight in design; geared towards the emerging mediums of type in the 1930s, such as art posters, magazines, and business cards, the focus is on modernist principles. But so much of the wisdom contained therein applies to designing space in a very abstract sense, and that is why I like it.
