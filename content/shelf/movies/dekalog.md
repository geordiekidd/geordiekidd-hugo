Some of the best yet most torturous cinema you will ever watch. This is a Polish series of ten hour-long films that are loosely based on the ten commandments, but in many ways come to constitute their own allegories. Human relationships, morality, consequence, love, despite, ego, fate, uncertainty; *Dekalog* has everything. Exceptionally written and directed. If I had to recommend only one work from KK, this would be it. Episodes 1 (about God) and 6 (about love) are stand-outs.
