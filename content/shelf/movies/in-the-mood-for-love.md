Well, what is there to say that has not elsewhere been said? A film of truly exceptional (motivic and visual) beauty, it captures so perfectly the tension, confusion, suppression, and painful longing of mis-fortunate love. Performances by Maggie Cheung and Tony Leung must rank amongst the best ever, owing of course much to the incredible screenplay which would be a favourite book of mine were it sold in that format. Each time I watch this movie I notice new things that ultimately deepen the characters and make their portrayal all the more impressive. I would be tempted to pick this for the desert island, although I'm not sure I could withstand a lifetime of heartbreak.
