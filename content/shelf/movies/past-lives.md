One of the most tragic movies I've seen, but I think for unexpected reasons. An honest film about two (or three, I suppose) people whose lives line up at the wrong times; an anti-fairy tale story, perhaps. *Past Lives* is one of the rarely perfect if-things-had-have-been-otherwise films, because it is so ordinary: childhood friends re-unite briefly, but are separated once more by the time printed on a plane ticket, each of them left wondering what might've been. It is anticlimactic and that's what makes it real, but also what makes it familiar and therefore tragic.
