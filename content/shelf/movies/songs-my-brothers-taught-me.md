The most recent film to render me a complete mess. Chloé Zhao's debut follows the lives of Johnny and his younger sister Jashuan living in Pine Ridge Indian Reservation in South Dakota, following the loss of their father and in the context of Johnny's planned departure to LA. The film, with a cast of amateurs and locals of Pine Ridge, traces the complex and destructive role of alcohol in the reservation overlaid with strenuous family and community dynamics. It offers a very tender look at siblinghood, family, and obligation. Wonderful.
