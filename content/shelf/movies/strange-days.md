A Y2K sci-fi crime thriller about an ex-cop who investigates a crime. Memories can be recorded from brain to disc and are sold on the black market for the voyeurism of the price-payer. One particular traded memory is of an especially heinous crime, which Lenny (the ex-cop) investigates. A very disturbing but completely enthralling picture. It's a dark and grungy essay on arousal, voyeurism, power, and technology, and although its Y2K theming is rather dated by today's standards, it's still a total thriller.
