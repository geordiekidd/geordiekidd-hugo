I've never seen any other film quite as visually stunning as this one. You can follow the metaphorically biographic story if you concentrate really intensely, or you can simply (and with far less effort) sit back and be mesmerised by its incomparable beauty. It is a poem about the life of Armenian poet Sayat-Nova, told as a series of largely self-subsistent scenes (chapters) populated with traditional Armenian textile, music, scenery, religion, and colour. Remarkable and true avant-garde cinema.
