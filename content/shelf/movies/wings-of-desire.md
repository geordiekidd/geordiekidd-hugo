This is my favourite movie of all time. A beautiful, somewhat enigmatic film about an angel whose stoic watching-over of people in Berlin develops in him a fascination with their complex and dynamic lives, so that all he desires is to become human and experience for himself what it means to feel. It is a modern fairytale of sorts. The colour/greyscale motif is the best I've ever witnessed; it is perfect.
