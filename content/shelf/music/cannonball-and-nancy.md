It doesn't really get much better than this, and by 'this' I mean straight-ahead vocal jazz. Nancy Wilson offers what should be considered the definitive vocal recordings of these tunes. An incredible voice, and even more incredible that she was only 24 at the time of recording. Credit also goes to the band, who not only maturely support Wilson on the first half of the album but go off on the instrumental tracks on the second.

Favourite song: *Save Your Love for Me*.
