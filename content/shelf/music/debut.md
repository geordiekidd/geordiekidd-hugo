To me this album is an absolutely timeless classic, even if it has some of that very identifiable early 90s electro-pop sound. There aren't many (or any?) other albums out there that sound quite like this one though. It has a very alluring and quirky aura, with its mixture of club-dance, folk, jazz, and pop styling. Bj&ouml;rk is my all time favourite singer and this is my favourite work of hers, by far, maybe because it played frequently at home when I was a kid.

Favourite songs: *Big Time Sensuality*, and *Like Someone in Love*.
