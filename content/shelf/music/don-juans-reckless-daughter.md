This is by far the best Joni Mitchell album, because of not only the sophisticated and eclectic set list but the image it so confidently paints, which is of a lover's journey through a very disorienting American desert. This album is not necessarily ear-pleasing in the same way as her popular ones (*Hejira*, *Blue*), in that it firmly rejects genre boundaries and instead pursues more mature musical and thematic adventures than her previous works. But I think that's exactly why I'm drawn to it. A companion album worth checking out is the largely-neglected *The Hissing of Summer Lawns*, released a few years prior.

Favourite songs: *Cotton Avenue*, and *The Tenth World*.
