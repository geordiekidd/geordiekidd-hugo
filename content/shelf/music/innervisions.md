I would never admit that I have just one favourite Stevie Wonder record, because I don't. Still, if I was to recommend only one, it would be *Innervisions*. Not only is the songwriting and harmonising nothing short of brilliant, but Stevie plays almost every instrument on every song. And that makes it even more surprising that the groove is _that_ good throughout. This album, and his other albums from around the same time, place Stevie Wonder undoubtedly near the top of the 'best composers of the 20th century' list.
