It goes toe-to-toe with *A Love Supreme*, although it is more akin to a straight-ahead jazz set with its mixture of standards and originals. Trane sings over every tune so lyrically. You really hear a large (giant?) step towards freedom, as if he is just then and there seeing the light. Englightening and psalmic, this music is important to my meditations on living, freedom, and dying. Maybe this is owed to the album's *Alabamba*, an elegy written for the victims of a racial attack only months earlier. Astounding.

Favourite track: *I Want to Talk About You*.
