Marvellous, majestic Meddle. It sets aside the cerebral (and admittedly difficult) sonic experimentation of their previous efforts like *Atom Heart Mother* to pursue a more poetic set that concentrates on themes of love, fate, existence, and purpose. Roger Waters' best lyrics for the group. And what could one enjoy more than the second-side-spanning adventure *Echoes*? Magnificent.

Favourite track: *Echoes*.
