Welcome

I’m Geordie [ˈd͡ʒoˑdi], and this is my personal website. I work on the phonology and morphology of the Anmatyerr dialect chain spoken around central Australia, with a focus on variation and word structure. This site I use as a personal blog, a repository of my work, and a record of my activities. All views are my own. Listen to some music while you browse.

If you’d like to get in contact with me, simply epenthesise [@gmail] at the obvious location in my website URL. Please avoid addressing me by surname. Fan or hate mail welcomed.
